## 🤝 Engagement Process

1. **Initial consultation:** clarify the business problem, intended users and desired outcome.
2. **Scope and proposal:** agree deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, price and payment milestones in writing.
3. **Secure data handover:** agree on an appropriate transfer method and access controls.
4. **Analysis and development:** provide progress updates at agreed milestones.
5. **Delivery and validation:** demonstrate outputs and check them against the agreed acceptance criteria.
6. **Revisions and support:** revision rounds and post-delivery support are those stated in the specific proposal or package agreement.
